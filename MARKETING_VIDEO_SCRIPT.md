## Feature Summary for Script Reference

### Offers & Discounts
- **Bill Discount** — % or flat off the total bill
- **Item Discount** — % or flat off specific menu items or categories
- **Promo Codes** — optional, customer enters code at checkout
- **Day scheduling** — active only on selected days (e.g. Mon–Wed)
- **Date range** — run offers for specific periods (e.g. Diwali week)
- **Usage limits** — cap how many times an offer can be redeemed
- **Runtime toggle** — enable/disable instantly from dashboard, reflects on customer cart immediately
- **Stats** — see how many times each offer was redeemed and total discount given

### Campaigns
- **4 campaign types** — Discount, New Dish, Festival, Custom
- **WhatsApp first, SMS fallback** — maximum reach guaranteed
- **Personalized messages** — use `{customerName}` and `{restaurantName}` variables
- **Audience** — auto-targets all customers who have ordered from the restaurant
- **Pay-per-send** — ₹1.38 per customer, no monthly marketing fee
- **Campaign history** — track status of every campaign sent

## Production Notes

- Use real screen recordings of the dashboard and customer menu — the UI looks polished
- Show the QR scan → order flow as the hero moment
- For offers: show the toggle ON/OFF and the instant effect on the customer cart — this is the wow moment
- For campaigns: show the audience count number — big numbers are impressive
- Use upbeat background music, no voiceover on the feature montage — let the product speak
- Mobile-first framing for customer scenes (phone in hand)
